Društvo za trgovinu i usluge sa sjedištem u Zagrebu. Razvijamo i održavamo PlayLoru — igru koja klasičnu Loru prenosi na internet. A trade and services company based in Zagreb, Croatia. We build and run PlayLora, which brings the classic card game Lora online.

Što radimoWhat we do

PlayLora

Lora za četvero igrača, online. Osam vrsta igara, 32 igre po partiji, statistika i natjecanja — bez miješanja karata rukom. Lora for four players, online. Eight game types, 32 games per match, statistics and competitions — with no shuffling by hand.
